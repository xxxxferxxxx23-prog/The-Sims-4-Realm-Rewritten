# Realm Rewritten: The Return of the Founders

## Premise
The player uncovers ancient founders of magic, discovering that the Realm’s power is fracturing due to misuse. Through quests, they must restore balance — or harness the chaos.

## Lore Anchors
- **Glimmerbrook** = the surface world hub
- **The Magic Realm** = multi-layered dimension with shifting biomes
- **Founders** = 4 legendary sages with conflicting ideologies
- **Romance paths** with NPCs (and rival spellcasters) affect magic affinity

## Core Lore
- The Realm’s energy is splintered across four elemental domains.
- Each domain grants new spells, aspirations, and unique romance events.
- Player choices in relationships and quests alter magical balance.
